## 🧩 Problem: Day of the Week

### Description

Given a positive integer **y**, determine which day of the week it represents.

The mapping is as follows:

* 1 → Sunday
* 2 → Monday
* 3 → Tuesday
* 4 → Wednesday
* 5 → Thursday
* 6 → Friday
* 7 → Saturday

Print the corresponding weekday name.

---

### Input

A single positive integer **y** (1 ≤ y ≤ 7).

---

### Output

Print the corresponding day of the week:

```
Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday
```
