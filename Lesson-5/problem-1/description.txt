
## 🧩 Problem: Mixed Caesar Cipher

### Description

You are given a single uppercase English letter (A–Z).

Apply a special encryption rule:

* If the letter is in the first half of the alphabet (A–M), shift it **forward by 3 positions**.
* If the letter is in the second half of the alphabet (N–Z), shift it **backward by 3 positions**.

The alphabet is circular, meaning after Z it wraps back to A.

Print the encrypted letter.

---

### Input

A single uppercase English letter (A–Z).

---

### Output

Print the encrypted letter.
