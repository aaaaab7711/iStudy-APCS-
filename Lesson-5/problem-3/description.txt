## 🧩 Problem: Days in a Month

### Description

Given a positive integer **y**, determine the number of days in the corresponding month.

Assume a non-leap year.

The rules are:

* Months with 31 days: 1, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12
* Months with 30 days: 4, 6, 9, 11
* February (2): 28 days

---

### Input

A single positive integer **y** (1 ≤ y ≤ 12).
